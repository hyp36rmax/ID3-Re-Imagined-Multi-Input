### Guardrail alignment correction

Evidence: `Verification/tsubaki-guardrail-20260927/`. A direct comparison against the rendered beam mesh found the missing mismatch, particularly at source points 3567–3587 before the hairpin itself. `rail-comparison.png` shows the original boundary crossing the road inside the rail; `rail-projected-offsets.json` records measured world positions and source triangles. `before.txt` shows the native regression failing 10 cm before the visible beam at point 3570.

`Tools/tsubaki_guardrails.py` extracts the actual corrugated beam edges from the guardrail atlas, excludes sponsor tiles, and restricts changes to the surveyed source range 3520–3640. It retains road heights and the route centre, adds quarter-metre collision sampling, and refines both sides of each beam endpoint to avoid extending an invisible diagonal into open pavement. The rebuilt RCL has 29,760 triangles, within its signed 16-bit limits. The two race-edge files match the corrected limits; authored scenery and `road.bin` remain unchanged.

`after.txt` covers 12 independent surveyed rail-clearance/contact/endpoint assertions, 120 real AE86 contact ticks beside the previously misplaced wall in both headings, and the existing whole-route surface, interior, cached and outward collision checks. `geometry-tests.txt` confirms unchanged route centre/heights/unaffected edges, valid RCL indices and identical guardrail geometry across all four weather/time variants. `render-fixed.log` and the new `verified/day_dry/*-edges.png` show the actual RCL edges against the real scenery; the overlay is raised 60 cm only for visibility.

The three corrected course files are staged into the local `Builds/Current` player. Subsequently, at the user's request, those same three files were installed into `C:/Users/Chris/Desktop/Current` and SHA-256 verified, with backups and an installation report in `Verification/tsubaki-guardrail-20260927/desktop-deployment.json`.
